About Me

Ghanshyam Patel, known online as GD, is an award-winning creative developer from Poland who builds interactive, engaging websites from scratch. Currently studying Computer Science at the University of Opole, he specializes in handcrafted web experiences with smooth animations, scroll-driven storytelling, and attention to detail that makes every site feel alive. His core toolkit includes React, Next.js, JavaScript, GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform), and CSS — the foundation for buttery-smooth page transitions, micro-interactions, and scroll-triggered motion. When a project calls for it, he also works with Three.js, WebGL, and custom shaders to create immersive browser-based experiences, but his heart is in clean, well-animated storytelling websites that people actually enjoy exploring. GD is not a designer — he doesn't work in Figma or Photoshop. He sketches ideas on paper and brings them to life directly in code, designing as he builds. He is also not a 3D modeler — he doesn't create models in Blender — but when clients provide ready assets (GLTF, GLB), he knows how to make them move, interact, and perform at 60fps in the browser. His work has earned international recognition including FWA Site of the Day, GSAP Site of the Day, CSS Design Awards, and Awwwards nominations. Beyond portfolio sites, his projects range from a webcam-controlled browser game using MediaPipe AI (67 Game) to premium commercial websites for photography studios with custom DOM virtualization handling 1000+ images. GD is available for freelance creative development projects worldwide.

Projects

  • Adam & Ewa – Premium Photography Website with Smooth Animations

    Adam & Ewa is a premium commercial website built from scratch by creative developer Ghanshyam Patel (GD) for a wedding photography and videography duo based in Poland. The project replaces a decade-old legacy site with a bespoke, template-free experience built on React Router v7, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, and Sanity headless CMS. It features an asymmetrical scattered gallery layout with parallax drift, FLIP-technique shared element lightbox transitions, scroll-driven typography animations with progressive blur reveal, and a custom double-layer DOM virtualization system using IntersectionObserver. This virtualization enables smooth 60fps scrolling through galleries containing up to 1000 high-resolution wedding photos — a massive engineering challenge solved entirely in frontend code. Images are optimized through Sanity's CDN with device-responsive sizing, WebP/AVIF auto-formatting, and LQIP blur-up placeholders. The site scores a perfect 100/100 on Google Lighthouse. Typography combines Cormorant Garamond serif headings with Inter body text on a warm eggshell background. The client manages all content independently through Sanity Studio.

  • 67 Game – Browser Webcam Game with Real-Time Pose Tracking

    67 Game is a browser-based speed challenge created by Ghanshyam Patel (GD) where your webcam becomes the controller. Players perform the viral '67' trend moves as fast as possible within 15 seconds while MediaPipe Pose Landmarker tracks wrist positions in real-time at 60fps — entirely in the browser with no downloads or plugins required. Built with React 19 and MediaPipe WASM running on GPU, the game features a global daily leaderboard powered by Firebase, auto-generated PDF certificates with QR codes, Canvas-based particle effects, and bilingual support in Polish and English. Debuted at the University of Opole Science Festival 2026 where over 100 people queued to play. Kids speedran attempts while adults were amazed that real-time computer vision runs natively in a web browser. A project that pushes the boundaries of creative frontend engineering.

  • Young Multi – Artist Website with Custom WebGL Image Distortion

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What makes GD different from other developers?

GD codes everything from scratch — no WordPress, no Webflow, no templates. His philosophy is "mechanics first": he perfects the layout, scroll behavior, and performance before adding any visual effects. This means every site runs smoothly at 60fps even on mobile phones. He combines award-winning creative execution with solid engineering fundamentals, delivering websites that look impressive and actually perform well under the hood.

What technologies does GD use?

GD's main tools are React, Next.js, JavaScript, GSAP (GreenSock) for animation, and CSS. For smooth scrolling he uses Lenis, and for content management Sanity headless CMS with Vercel hosting. When projects require it, he also works with Three.js, React Three Fiber, and WebGL for interactive browser-based experiences. He codes everything from scratch — no templates, no page builders.

Who is GD and what does he do?

GD is the creative alias of Ghanshyam Patel, a frontend developer from Poland who builds interactive, handcrafted websites from scratch. He focuses on smooth animations, scroll-driven storytelling, and clean code — creating sites that feel alive and engaging. His work ranges from animated portfolio websites to webcam-controlled browser games, and has been recognized with awards including FWA Site of the Day and CSS Design Awards.

What kind of websites does GD build?

GD builds a range of interactive websites — from storytelling portfolios with smooth scroll animations and page transitions, to immersive artist sites with custom visual effects, to clean commercial websites for businesses that need premium aesthetics and fast performance. He prefers projects where animation and user experience tell a story rather than just display content statically. Every project is coded from scratch with no templates.

What is 67 Game and how does it work?

67 Game is a browser-based speed challenge at sixsevengame.vercel.app where your webcam is the controller. You perform the viral '67' trend moves as fast as possible in 15 seconds while MediaPipe tracks your wrists in real-time — entirely in the browser with zero downloads needed. Built with React 19 and MediaPipe WASM on GPU. Features include a global daily leaderboard via Firebase, auto-generated PDF certificates with QR codes, particle effects, and Polish/English language support. Over 100 people queued to play it at a university science festival.

Does GD take freelance projects?

Yes. GD is available for freelance creative development projects worldwide. He works best with clients who have a clear vision or provide designs he can bring to life with animation and code. His sweet spot is storytelling websites with smooth interactions, artist and portfolio sites, and premium business websites. He quotes fixed-price estimates after understanding the full project scope and typically delivers within 4 to 8 weeks.

Has GD won any web design awards?

Yes. GD's projects have received multiple international awards including FWA Site of the Day, GSAP Site of the Day, CSS Design Awards Special Kudos, and Awwwards nominations. His Young Multi artist website earned CSSDA Site of the Day and Design Nominees Site of the Day. These awards recognize both the creative quality and technical performance of his work.

Is GD a designer or a 3D modeler?

No. GD is a developer, not a designer or 3D artist. He doesn't work in Figma, Photoshop, or Blender. He sketches layout ideas on paper and then builds directly in code, designing as he goes. When clients provide finished UI designs or ready 3D models (GLTF/GLB files), he can implement and animate them with precision — but the creation of those assets is not his role.
